# Cedar case-record retention — synthetic evaluation source

## Current schedule

Urgent case logs are retained for 180 days after closure. Standard case logs
are retained for 60 days after closure. The records steward owns deletion.
The case identifier must be retained in the deletion audit trail.

## Preservation exception

If a case is under a preservation hold, deletion is suspended even after the
normal retention period expires. The compliance lead releases the hold in
writing. After release, the records steward applies the original closure-based
schedule; release does not restart the retention clock.

## Missing information

This policy specifies no backup retention period and no monetary deletion fee.
